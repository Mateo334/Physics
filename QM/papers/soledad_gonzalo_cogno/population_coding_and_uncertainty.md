# Population coding and model uncertainty

## Nagelhus et al.: object-centered coding
- **Bibliography:** Anne Nagelhus, Sebastian O. Andersson, Soledad Gonzalo Cogno, Edvard I. Moser, May-Britt Moser, *Object-centered population coding in CA1 of the hippocampus*, Neuron 111(13), 2091–2104.e14 (2023), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2023.04.008.
- **Version actually read:** 2022 preprint full HTML, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.07.07.499197v1.full ; saved as `Nagelhus_et_al_2022_Object_centered_preprint_web.txt`. Published metadata/abstract checked separately. Final-paper and preprint PDF requests returned HTTP 403; no PDF was obtained. Do not attribute preprint-specific methods/numbers to the final version without checking it.
- **Locations checked:** preprint Introduction, Results, Discussion, Figs. 1–7 and supplementary figure captions 6–7; STAR Methods Bayesian decoding algorithm and word/count analyses. HTML has no verified printed pagination. Formula images were not transcribed: equations in the introduction are conventional restatements of the verbal methods.
- **[From source] Setup:** CA1 extracellular spike recordings in freely moving rats, using tetrodes and Neuropixels; compare no object, object present, object moved, and object removed. A place cell has spatially localized firing; an object-vector cell has a preferred displacement relative to an object.
- **[From source] Result:** heterogeneous single-cell changes combine into a population response whose reorganization is strongest near the object, decreases gradually with distance, and lacks a preferred direction. Population-vector correlations and object-presence decoding show this graded effect. Increasing cell numbers improves its visibility, including when selecting weakly distance-tuned cells. This is distributed functional coding, not a direct measurement of anatomical connectivity or a proof of high-order synergy.
- **Decoder:** conditional-independent Poisson spike counts given position and trial condition; rate maps trained on one half of a trial and tested on the other, then swapped. One-second windows. Likelihood is marginalized over position using the occupancy prior before comparing equal-prior object/no-object conditions. Independence is an inference-model assumption, not established independence of actual neurons. Controls include shuffled labels, occupancy-only decoding, fixed distance bins, and within-trial time comparisons.
- **Words:** count vector in 10-ms windows, mostly zero/one but not formally restricted to binary; compare with total count. Neuropixels data subsampled to 30 cells for this analysis, not the entire largest recorded population. All-zero word excluded; 50 trial-label shuffles estimate information bias. Word identity carries object-condition information not visible in total counts. Frequency distributions change word identities rather than simply their sorted shape or number.
- **Caveats:** finite-sample information estimates and exclusion of silence matter; apparent many-neuron coding does not establish that cell-cell correlations are indispensable. Mutual information about condition is not KS entropy or entropy-production rate. Qualitative published abstract agrees with the main preprint result, but final full-text details were not verified.

## Simmonds et al.: model-related uncertainty
- **Bibliography:** Emily G. Simmonds et al. (including Soledad Gonzalo Cogno), *Insights into the quantification and reporting of model-related uncertainty across different disciplines*, iScience 25(12), 105512 (2022), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2022.105512.
- **Local PDF:** `Simmonds_et_al_2022_Model_related_uncertainty.pdf`; extracted `.txt` alongside it.
- **Open source:** https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/83068/7/Simmonds_etal_iScience_2022_Insights_into_the_quantification_and_reporting_of_model_related_uncertainty.pdf
- **Read:** main text pp. 1–14, especially framework pp. 1–4, Box 2 pp. 4–5, recommendations and limitations p. 14. References not individually checked.
- **[From source] Result:** systematic audit across seven fields; 545 assessed papers, 480 included. Framework separates response-variable data, explanatory-variable data, parameters, and model structure. No field consistently covers all relevant sources; uncertainty in explanatory data is especially often unreported. The study assesses reporting, not the quality or correctness of every uncertainty estimate.
- **Recommendations:** propagate relevant measurement and parameter uncertainty, examine alternative models, present numerical intervals and visual summaries, carry uncertainty into conclusions, and explicitly discuss omitted sources. Not every fixed-parameter theoretical calculation needs artificial parameter error bars.
- **Limitations:** unequal field sample sizes, possible reviewer variation, limited social-science coverage, and no assessment of how omissions changed conclusions. This is a methodology paper, not a neural dynamical model.

Checked 2026-11-24. No experimental reanalysis was performed.
