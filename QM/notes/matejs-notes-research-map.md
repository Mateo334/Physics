## 1. Golden braid and the unitary obstruction

**Source:** “Golden braid” / “Golden braid resolution,” lines 1732–1744; “Hamiltonian equations lyapunov exponents,” 1825–1924; “Lyapunov differential equation necessities,” 2051–2063.

The classical target is:

```latex
\dot z=J\nabla H(z),\qquad\dot{\delta z}=JD^2H(z(t))\delta z.
```

The notebook correctly observes that the exact density-matrix tangent instead obeys the same linear commutator equation as the reference state:

```latex
\dot\rho=-i[H,\rho],\qquad\dot{\delta\rho}=-i[H,\delta\rho].
```

- [Proved, finite dimension] Common unitary conjugation preserves Hilbert–Schmidt and other unitarily invariant norms, giving zero state-space tangent exponents. Pure-state Fubini–Study geometry does not evade this.
- [Clarification from current discussion] For initial-condition sensitivity, the perturbation equation must be the derivative of the base evolution. More generally one needs a specified linear cocycle and norm; two unrelated evolution equations do not suffice. Hamiltonian form is a special case, not a universal prerequisite.
- [Correction] The notebook's proposed necessity of nonlinear dynamics is too strong: linear unstable systems can have positive exponents, without thereby being chaotic.
- [Scope] OE is a scalar readout, QFI is a metric for a specified parameter encoding, and energy uncertainty gives pure-state physical-time speed. None alone defines a closed directional perturbation dynamics. Common parameter-independent unitary evolution preserves initial-parameter QFI; time-independent H preserves energy uncertainty.
- Existing checked discussion: [tangent geometry](theorems/quantum-hamiltonian-tangent-geometry.md), [OE/QFI/Pesin](theorems/observational-entropy-qfi-pesin.md). These are partial results, not acceptance of the previous direction by the user.

## 5. Cone generation does not automatically saturate observational entropy
