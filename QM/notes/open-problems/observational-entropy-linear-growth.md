# Fixed-POVM observational entropy: intermediate linear growth

[Proved: entropy deficit identity] For fixed effects E_a, p_a=Tr(rho E_a), v_a=Tr(E_a)>0 and q_a=v_a/d, observational entropy equals log(d)-D_KL(p||q). Here q is the outcome distribution of I/d. For our plot, which averages entropy values over states and PVMs, the deficit from log(d) is the average of these measurement-distribution divergences, not the divergence of averaged probabilities and not a minimum over arbitrary state pairs. Minimizing relative entropy over all state pairs gives zero (also an infimum of zero for distinct pairs). A lower observed plateau can be represented by a nonzero late-time average deficit, but no convergence to its minimum follows. Finite-dimensional unitary dynamics need not have a strict pointwise entropy limit. A typical-state reference or finite-time plateau estimate must be labeled as such.

Source clarification: checked `papers/2212.01585v3.pdf`, pp. 5–6. Its Fig. 3(b) is a straight-line test of log(log(d)-OE), hence exponential deficit relaxation, not a sustained affine entropy law. Fig. 4 separately uses the short-time OE slope at the third kick. Thus this paper does not establish the proposed general linear-growth premise. See `../papers/observational-entropy-kicked-top.md` for measurements and caveats.

[Conjecture] For suitable initially localized semiclassical states and specified fixed, semiclassically meaningful POVMs, ensemble-averaged observational entropy may have an approximately linear intermediate-time window, after an initial transient and before saturation or breakdown of the applicable semiclassical approximation. No theorem or universal coefficient is established here. Interpret “linear growth” as entropy affine in time (constant slope), not slope affine in time.

[Proved: obstruction to universality] Chaos alone cannot imply positive growth for arbitrary fixed POVMs. For discrete unitary dynamics U, if every effect E_a commutes with U, then Tr(U^n rho U^{-n} E_a)=Tr(rho E_a); volumes are fixed, so observational entropy is constant for every initial state. Floquet spectral projectors provide such a PVM, and their trace-weighted unsharp versions provide nonprojective examples. The continuous-time counterpart uses effects commuting with H. This obstruction applies regardless of chaotic diagnostics of the dynamics. A stationary initial state also has constant entropy for any fixed POVM.

[Heuristic] Exponentially increasing numbers of comparably weighted, equal-volume occupied measurement cells would give approximately linear entropy. Chaotic stretching alone does not establish this premise: folding, projection onto a measured coordinate, nonuniform probabilities, and measurement resolution matter. No identification of the entropy slope with a Lyapunov exponent or KS entropy has been proved.

[Unresolved] Specify the allowed POVMs, their scaling with spin j, the ensemble, the transient and saturation criteria, and an error notion for linearity. Saturation time and Ehrenfest time are distinct. Existing j=200, 20-state mean curves bend toward plateaus; there is no fitted or parametrically controlled linear window yet. See `notes/calculations/kicked-top-observational-entropy.md` for parameters and sampling caveats. No new simulations or literature checks in this discussion.
