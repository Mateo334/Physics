## Results and scope
- [Proved] Pure quantum states form projective Hilbert space with an exact Hamiltonian flow of the expectation Hamiltonian. With g=2ħ Re and omega=2ħ Im, complex structure I=i and contraction omega(X,.)=dh, X=−I grad h and the covariant tangent equation is ∇t eta=−I Hess_g h[eta]. This is a variational equation, not a geodesic Jacobi equation.
- [Proved] Quantum expectation Hamiltonians generate Killing fields. Common unitary evolution preserves FS tangent norms; finite-dimensional state-space FS exponents vanish. Density-matrix tangents likewise transform by unitary conjugation and preserve unitarily invariant norms. This does not rule out observable-response growth.
- [Proved] Classical distribution divergences also remain invariant under common invertible transport; comparing distributions instead of physical phase-space coordinates already changes the classical question.
- [Proved, algebraic] For canonical R, let G_b=sum_c (J^-1)_cb R_c and M_ab=[R_a(t),G_b]/(iħ). Then M(0)=I, and its expectation is the exact response to an initial displacement generated by G_b. For scalar-coefficient noncommutative polynomial Heisenberg equations dot R=F(R), dot M=DF(R)[M] columnwise. DF inserts each variation into each ordered monomial slot. Quartic force gives −g(Q²V+QVQ+VQ²), not −3gQ²V.
- [Proved] Quadratic Hamiltonians yield the ordinary classical tangent matrix exactly, times the identity operator.
- [Conditional semiclassical statement] Derivative-controlled Egorov plus localization and moment/product bounds recover M_classical and its Gram at fixed time. Commuting an exact Weyl symbol with a linear generator differentiates it exactly; substituting the classical trajectory for that quantum symbol is the approximation. No general long-time remainder theorem established.
- [Definition, not theorem] Real-part state-weighted response Gram describes real initial displacements. It is distinct from the Gram of mean responses and can have a different spectrum from the complex Hermitian Gram. Physical coordinate metrics must be fixed.
- [Proved example] Inverted oscillator in canonical dimensionless units has centroid/operator tangent growth exp(t), while covariance grows too and FS distance/displacement QFI stay constant. It is integrable and unstable, not an example proving chaos.
- [From source] Variational/coherent-state projection can break isometry; exact invariance of the chosen manifold must not be assumed. Non-Kähler and symplectic-degeneracy issues require additional care.

## Limitations / build
Unbounded-operator equations assume a common invariant core and sufficient moments; these hypotheses were not proved for arbitrary polynomial Hamiltonians. No nonlinear chaotic-model numerical comparison, no universal quantum Pesin relation, no identification with ALF/CNT/OE, and no asymptotic exponent from finite-size transient growth.
