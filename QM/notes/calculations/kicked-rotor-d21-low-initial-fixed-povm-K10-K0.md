## Model and objective

Periodic q in [0,2pi); d=21 grid q_j=2pi*j/21, discrete Fourier momentum modes m=-10,...,10 (cyclic modular momentum), p_m=hbar_eff*m, hbar_eff=2pi/21=0.2991993003, |p|<=10*hbar_eff=2.99199. Periodized position-space minimal-uncertainty Gaussian centered q0=pi/2, p0=0 with sigma_q=sqrt(hbar_eff/2), normalized and transformed to momentum. Initial psi at n=0; full unmeasured Floquet `psi_(n+1)=FFT[e^(-i K cos(q)/hbar_eff) IFFT(e^(-i p²/(2 hbar_eff)) psi_n)]`; for K=0 use same preparation and free Floquet. Post-kick n=1,2,3 densities are pure; one fixed four-effect POVM per K, with `E_i>=0`, `sum E_i=I21` and probabilities `p_i(n)=<psi_n,E_i psi_n>`. Natural-log observational entropy `S_n=-sum_i p_i(n)log(p_i(n)/Tr E_i)`, bounded above by ln21=3.04452244.

Optimization: `T=3`, OLS slope `R=sum_n (n-1.5)S_n/5`, residual `e_n=S_n-S_0-nR`; `J=R-mu*sum_n e_n²-gamma*S_0²-beta*max(0,S_3-(ln21-margin))²`. User did not supply hyperparameters; **chosen** `mu=1`, `gamma=10`, `beta=20`, `margin=0.35` nats. The last optional *extra* penalty discourages end-window saturation (not a hard constraint): final gap is 0.34167 rather than strictly >=0.35. Initially six independent random/aligned POVMs were tried per K (seed 20261129+100+K); final four starts are **correlated perturbations of saved POVMs**, not four additional independent initializations. Analytical gradient of both observational entropy and all penalty terms, checked at random interior effects to relative ~1e-8 and for 2%-mixed final effects to absolute ~8e-9 (active saturation term K10) and ~5e-8 (K0). Backtracked projected gradient ascent with Dykstra Euclidean projection on full complex Hermitian 21x21 four-effect tuples. Strictly positive final effects after 1e-8 uniform mixture; minimum eigenvalues 2.5e-9 (K10), 2.39e-9 (K0), sum-to-identity Frobenius error below 1e-15; outcome sums verified below 1e-9. All runs reproducible with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1; py -3.13 calculations/code/kicked_rotor_d21_low_initial_fixed_povm.py --restarts 4 --steps 900 --beta 20 --margin .35 --warm-start calculations/results/kicked_rotor_d21_K10_K0_lowS0_T3_margin035_warm_r4s400.npz --tag margin035_refine_r4s900`.

## Cutoff and Ehrenfest limitations

[Numerically tested] At K=10 and d21 the probability in the outer 20% of available momentum modes after kicks 1,2,3 is 0.519,0.214,0.139, respectively. Repeating the *same physical preparation and hbar_eff* in d257 and d513, the fraction outside the 21-mode physical cutoff |m|<=10 is **0.9761,0.3527,0.8460** after kicks 1,2,3, almost identical for d257/513; d21 evolution therefore experiences severe momentum aliasing from the first kick. K0 initial outside-21 mass in d513 stays ~1.2e-16, and momentum energy is conserved. The K10 numerical result is valid for the chosen finite torus, **not** a cutoff-independent unbounded K10 rotor simulation. In particular the comparison of K10 vs K0 optimized slopes (0.911 vs 0.735) does not diagnose chaos: the K0 system already gives positive optimized entropy growth under free dispersion, and the K10 state suffers aliasing.

[Proved] `S_obs<=ln21`; a straight line of slope 1.619 starting from S0~0 reaches 4.857 at kick 3, impossible in d21. The chosen K10 curve stays ln21-S3=0.34167 away from saturation at the last sampled kick, although the saturating ceiling still limits possible rates. A heuristic Ehrenfest estimate log(d)/1.619≈1.88 kicks (or log(1/hbar_eff)/1.619≈0.75 kicks) is **shorter than the three-kick fit**; convention-dependent and not a certified Ehrenfest time.
