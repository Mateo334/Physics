# Instantaneous kicked-top OE with 20 random pure and mixed preparations

[Numerically tested] Four-outcome, one fixed POVM shared by **all 20 independently drawn preparations for each K**, maximizing the arithmetic mean of 20 **individual** instantaneous OE derivatives, not the OE derivative of the averaged density matrix. Same preparations for K=3,6. j=10,d=21, same Floquet cycle, rotation-phase t0=1.1, H=pi Jy as the earlier single-state study. Ten directions sphere-uniform (`cos(theta)` uniform [-1,1] and `phi` uniform [0,2pi]) yield pure coherent states; ten independently sampled directions yield full-rank mixed states `rho=eta|theta,phi><theta,phi|+(1-eta)I/21` with eta uniform [0.25,0.75]. Preparation seed 20261121 and optimizer seed 20261122; exact angles and mixing coefficients saved in summary. 10 independent POVM restarts for each K, no measurement backaction. Not Haar-random pure/mixed states and not 20 mixed + 20 pure. Full-rank mixed states have no canonical single classical Lyapunov trajectory.

Code `calculations/code/kicked_top_ensemble_mixed_povm.py` (90 iterations per restart), `calculations/code/refine_kicked_top_ensemble_mixed.py` (one warm start from best, up to 550 more iterations), `calculations/code/finalize_kicked_top_ensemble_mixed.py` (strict feasibility, final figure); all under `calculations/code/` and using existing `kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_pga.py`. Run in this order with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` and `py -3.13`. Files in `calculations/results/` with prefix `kicked_top_ensemble20_mixed_j10_m4`: original K3/K6 NPZ, `_summary.json`, refinement K3/K6 NPZ and `_refined_summary.json` (the latter only contains K6 because the retry preserved the earlier successful K3 file), **final** K3/K6 NPZ, `_final_summary.json`, `_final_comparison.png`. Do not rerun finalization without choosing new paths, since it refuses to overwrite.

Initial 90-step runs achieved only 11.69436 and 16.31290, with residuals 0.72 and 1.22, so must **not** be misreported as converged results. Best-rate restarts at 90 steps were restart 4 and 7. The K3 warm start ran all 550 refinements and retained residual 0.0056; K6 stopped after 300 refinements with residual 0.0011. Even these are not certified global optima. Some individual rates are negative (K3 min -0.361, K6 min -0.201), although their 20-state means are positive. The optimized pure/mixed subgroup means are at the single POVM optimized for all 20, not separately optimized subgroup rates.

Checks: coherent-state norm/PSD/trace, full rank of mixed states, unitary evolution, Hermitian trace-zero density derivatives, batch gradient equals 20 individual analytical gradients, finite-direction gradient checks ~1.4e-9 (K3), ~1.3e-10 (K6), fixed-POVM entropy centered-difference slopes 13.05793656 and 18.00498366 within 1e-8 of analytic rates. The Euclidean projection used the Dykstra fallback because CVXPY is unavailable. The original K6 refinement had a smallest effect eigenvalue ~-1.7e-8 due to finite projector tolerance; its first strict PSD assertion **failed**, not a solver success. Finalization rescales each refined effect as `E_i -> (1-10^-6)E_i+10^-6 I/4`, changing the rates by 3.3e-5 and 4.6e-5, and gives strictly positive minimum eigenvalues >=2.3e-7, completeness errors <=7e-16. The final PNG was inspected visually.

Interpretation: Unlike the individually tailored pure-state case, there is one POVM per K shared across 20 preparations; no simple pure-state `C_{d,m}Delta H` proportionality governs this mixed-ensemble mean. However, observed rates still depend on chosen initial states, their mixing, the imposed driving-cycle phase and choice of four unrestricted outcomes; a local rate is not an asymptotic/linear-regime entropy-production rate or a classical Lyapunov exponent. No ensemble-wide Ehrenfest-time claim was verified; different directions may have distinct classical exponents, and mixed states need a specified semiclassical phase-space distribution.
