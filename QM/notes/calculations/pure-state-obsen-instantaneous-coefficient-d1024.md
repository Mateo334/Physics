# Canonical unrestricted pure-state instantaneous observational-entropy coefficient

[Proved] At a fixed differentiable unitary time for a pure state, write `dot rho=DeltaH(|1><0|+|0><1|)` in an orthonormal basis for the state and its tangent, with DeltaH>0; changing the phase of |1> covers the sign. Under all POVMs with exactly m nonzero outcomes, `sup_E R(E)=C_{d,m} DeltaH`, independent of whether the underlying evolution is a kicked rotor or kicked top. If the rotor Hilbert space is infinite, specifying d=1024 is a truncation; the coefficient refers to that finite-dimensional problem and says nothing about a specific rotor Hamiltonian's DeltaH. Twirl any POVM by all unitaries on the orthogonal `(d-2)`-dimensional complement: twirling leaves `p_i`, `dot p_i`, `Tr E_i`, PSD and completeness unchanged, giving **without loss of generality** `E_i=A_i direct-sum alpha_i I_(d-2)` where `sum A_i=I_2`, A_i PSD, alpha_i>=0 and sum alpha_i=1. This is an exact full-POVM reduction, not a rank-one/phase-space restriction. Standalone explanatory file `latex/obsen_instantaneous_unrestricted_reduction.tex` (NO pdflatex/tectonic installed, NOT compile-tested).

[Numerically tested] Code `calculations/code/canonical_instantaneous_obsen_coefficient.py`, run `py -3.13 calculations/code/canonical_instantaneous_obsen_coefficient.py --dimension 1024 --outcomes 10 --restarts 16 --iterations 1500`, one-thread OpenBLAS recommended. Exact reduced full-HS Euclidean projection: Dykstra projection onto ten 2x2 PSD matrices summing to I2 and independent ordinary simplex projection of complement weights; the scalar gradient is understood in the full HS metric with complement multiplicity d-2. Canonical `rho=|0><0|`, `dotrho=-(|0><1|+|1><0|)`, DeltaH=1. Seed 20261125; saved `calculations/results/canonical_obsen_coefficient_d1024_m10.{json,npz}`. The NPZ stores a **compact exact representation** of the 1024x1024 POVM, not dense matrices.

[Proved lower bound, numerically optimized one-dimensional parameter] A two-outcome rank-one PVM `P=|u><u|`, `I-P`, with `|u>=sqrt(p)|0>+sqrt(1-p)|1>` for the chosen negative tangent, gives `R=2 sqrt(p(1-p)) log((d-1)p/(1-p))`. Splitting `I-P` proportionally into nine nonzero effects retains the same entropy and derivative and makes a ten-outcome POVM. Numerically maximize over p: for d=1024, `p=0.633713508662...`, `R=7.206288499393892`; agrees to floating point with all 16 reduced-space optimizer starts. This is an **explicit rigorous attained lower bound** on the global coefficient, not a proven exact optimum. Finite differences and convergence do not rule out a distinct, better ten-outcome POVM.
