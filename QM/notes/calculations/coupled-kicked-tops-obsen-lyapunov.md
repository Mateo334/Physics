# Coupled kicked tops: observational entropy versus classical positive exponents

[Numerically tested] Standalone script: `calculations/code/coupled_kicked_tops_obsen_lyapunov.py`. Output: `calculations/results/coupled_kicked_tops_obsen_lyapunov.png`. Run from workspace root: `py -3.13 calculations/code/coupled_kicked_tops_obsen_lyapunov.py`; optionally set `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`. Uses NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib already available in the Python 3.13 environment. Seed 20261116.

Quantum setup: two spins of j=12 (total Hilbert-space dimension d^2=625), each initially in a spin-coherent pure state with independent uniformly sampled sphere directions, 8 product preparations. One kick consists of R_y(pi/2) on each spin followed by `exp[-i kappa (Jz1^2+Jz2^2)/(2j)-i g Jz1 Jz2/j]`, with kappa=4, g=1, through 14 kicks. The fixed product POVM is built from spin-coherent projectors on Gauss-Legendre polar nodes (j+1 per spin) and 2j+1 evenly spaced azimuths, with quadrature weights. Its single-spin effects sum to I (numerically asserted at 1e-11). For the product effects E_ab, `p_ab=Tr(rho E_ab)`, `v_ab=Tr(E_ab)` and `S_obs=-sum_ab p_ab log(p_ab/v_ab)`. The plotted quantity averages the eight *individual* S_obs values, not their probability arrays. POVM is evaluated on fresh copies at each time and does not feed back into the dynamics. Probability normalization, pure-state norm and entropy range [0,2 log(2j+1)] are checked.

Classical map: apply Ry(pi/2) to each unit sphere vector, then rotate spin 1 around z by `kappa z1+g z2` and spin 2 by `kappa z2+g z1`, where z1,z2 are the post-Ry coordinates. Analytical 6x6 Jacobian, restricted to the 4D product-sphere tangent spaces, is checked against a central finite difference at h=1e-6, absolute tolerance 1e-7. QR estimates use 1000 classical kicks along each of the same eight initial directions. The ensemble-averaged ordered exponents are approximately `(0.74004, 0.50247, -0.50304, -0.73948)` per kick, with positive sum `1.24252` per kick. Finite-time ± pairing provides a sanity check; no rigorous asymptotic exponent claim is made.

Using the **heuristic** Ehrenfest convention `t_E=log(j)/mean(lambda_max)`, t_E≈3.358 kicks. A least-squares line through the only three integer points t=1,2,3 has slope ≈1.00526 nats/kick, versus the classical positive sum ≈1.24252. The fitted slope is sensitive to the transient and uses only three data points; this is neither an equality nor evidence of a well-developed linear regime. The plot visually saturates after roughly 8–10 kicks; saturation time is not the Ehrenfest time. The POVM choice, j scaling, ensemble size, classical convergence and fit window must be varied before a semiclassical correspondence claim.

## Current version: 100 initial states and a product PGM

The script was changed to use 100 independently and uniformly sampled product-coherent initial directions with the same seed, and an explicitly normalized phase-space *pretty-good measurement* (PGM) on each spin. Given weighted coherent-state columns `v_a`, its single-spin effects are `E_a=S^(-1/2)|v_a><v_a|S^(-1/2)` with `S=sum_a|v_a><v_a|`; the two-top effects are `E_a tensor E_b`. For this exact spherical quadrature `S≈I`, so the numerical PGM normalization changes the older coherent POVM only at roundoff level: the principal change is ensemble size, **not** a genuinely different measurement. The common POVM is fixed in time; it does not disturb quantum evolution. Lyapunov exponents are estimated along the same 100 starting directions with 1000 QR steps each. The comparison uses the mean over trajectories of each trajectory's sum of positive exponents, not the positive part of an averaged exponent. The earlier eight-state image `calculations/results/coupled_kicked_tops_obsen_lyapunov.png` remains historical.
