# Instantaneous kicked-top OE derivative: unrestricted four-outcome POVM

[Numerically tested] `calculations/code/kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_pga.py`; output `calculations/results/kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_j10_{summary.json,comparison.png,K3.npz,K6.npz}`. Run with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` and `py -3.13 calculations/code/kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_pga.py`. NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.16.3, Matplotlib 3.10.8; CVXPY absent from Python 3.13 and 3.10 and *not installed*. Code provides CVXPY/SCS semidefinite Euclidean projection when available; actual run used Dykstra's Euclidean projection onto the product PSD cone intersected with sum E=I. Dykstra is a fallback, not a claim that the requested CVXPY solver was run. No external source needed.

Spin j=10, d=21; four general PSD effects, one fixed POVM per K, independently initialized 10 times (seed 20261120). Initial |theta=1.1,phi=0.7> is a spin coherent state. Each cycle comprises H_R=pi Jy for first half time unit and H_T=K Jz²/j for second half; hence Floquet U=exp(-i K Jz²/(2j)) exp(-i pi Jy/2). Evaluate at t0=1.1, after a complete cycle and 0.1 unit into the next rotation. The relevant generator is H_R=pi Jy, not the Floquet logarithm; ρ and -i[H_R,ρ] remain fixed throughout optimization. No observation backaction. Instantaneous rate R=-sum_i dot p_i log(p_i/V_i) and analytic gradient G_i= -log(p_i/V_i) dot ρ -(dot p_i/p_i)ρ +(dot p_i/V_i)I. Computation requires p,V>1e-13 for a valid trial; *no probability clipping*. Accepted projected steps increase the true R. Random PSD initial effects normalized by a common inverse square root and slightly mixed with I/4.

Classical map of sphere (x,y,z) after rotation and twist: (z cos(Kx)+y sin(Kx), -z sin(Kx)+y cos(Kx), -x). Tangent-vector QR/rescaling along the **specified initial classical direction**, 20,000 steps, no burn-in; initial tangent is increasing theta. Largest finite-time exponent at 100/1000/10000/20000 iterations: K3 = 0.238011/0.323517/0.338536/0.338622, K6 = 0.983729/0.967619/0.974309/0.976162, in inverse kicks. tE=ln(10)/lambda≈6.79988 (K3), 2.35881 (K6). Numerically checked explicit map Jacobian against centered 1e-6 differences (largest error ~4.5e-11). Ehrenfest estimates have convention-dependent order-one factors, classical nonuniformity and j=10 limitations; t0=1.1 is **not securely separated** from tE for K6. The kicked top's classical phase space is the two-dimensional sphere, but its quantum Hilbert space here has dimension 21.

The K6/K3 rate contrast is **not** a Lyapunov/KS correspondence. A local slope can far exceed the total entropy range ln(21) per time unit and reverse nearby (see the fixed-POVM entropy plot). It is tailored independently to each state and instantaneous generator. Results are reproducible **lower bounds on the global maximum** over four-outcome POVMs, not a proof of global or high-precision stationarity; projected-gradient residuals ~0.003 are small compared with the 23–56 rate scale, but nonzero. CVXPY-backed numerical verification remains undone.

[Proved] For a **pure** ρ=|ψ><ψ| under a fixed Hamiltonian H, the pair (ρ, dotρ) is, up to unitary conjugation, entirely characterized by d and ΔH=sqrt(<H²>-<H>²): write |v>=-i(H-<H>)|ψ>, so dotρ=|v><ψ|+|ψ><v|, <ψ|v>=0 and ||v||=ΔH. Conjugation bijects all m-outcome POVMs; scaling dotρ scales the rate. Therefore the unrestricted m-outcome instantaneous supremum is C_{d,m} ΔH, not intrinsically a Lyapunov exponent. From saved best-run states, Δ(π Jy)=6.5488856426 for K3 and 15.9090353222 for K6, and achieved R/ΔH=3.5460434101 and 3.5460434610, respectively (within numerical solver tolerance). This is a stronger explanation for the observed rate contrast than a chaos comparison. It is specific to pure states, fixed phase/generator and unconstrained POVMs; do not extend it to restricted measurements or sustained growth without further work.

[Proved] Proportionally split one effect E into seven equal effects E/7. The resulting four-to-ten-outcome POVM has the **same** OE and derivative since p/7 and V/7 have the same ratio p/V and their contributions sum. Thus the exact optimum for ten outcomes is at least that for four. Verified numerically with saved four-outcome best candidates (differences below 1e-11), and preserved their explicit ten-effect lifts in `calculations/results/kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_j10_m10_K{3,6}_lifted_from_m4.npz`; comparison JSON `kicked_top_instantaneous_povm_j10_m10_lift_comparison.json`. No significant improvement from additional outcomes in this run, but that is not an equality theorem for optimal rates.
