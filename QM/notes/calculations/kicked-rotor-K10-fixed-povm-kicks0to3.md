# One fixed four-outcome POVM over the full K=10 rotor evolution to kick 3

[Numerically tested] This **corrects the objective**, not the previously published initial-instant POVMs: `calculations/code/kicked_rotor_fixed_povm_0_to_3_optimize.py`, run with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1; py -3.13 calculations/code/kicked_rotor_fixed_povm_0_to_3_optimize.py`. Outputs in `calculations/results/kicked_rotor_K10_fixed_povm_kicks0to3_m4.{json,npz,png}`. Saved arrays are a compact exact POVM representation, not full dxd effects. Results are not overwritten by rerunning. Plot visually inspected.

One coherent pure Gaussian initially at t=0 with q0=pi/2,p0=0,sigma_q=sqrt(hbar_eff/2), hbar_eff=2pi/64. K=10 delta kicks at t=1,2,3, with free momentum evolution p²/(2hbar_eff) between; compare d64 torus (severe alias) and d768 cutoff with same physical hbar (near-zero momentum edge weight through kick 3). **One POVM** is chosen at t=0 and kept unchanged through all three kicks and free intervals: no reoptimization or measurement backaction. Objective: ordinary least-squares slope of four **post-kick stroboscopic** OE values S(n^+), n=0,1,2,3, where n=0 is preparation (not a kick). Weights on these four entropies are (-.3,-.1,.1,.3). This is a finite-time fitted rate, not instantaneous dS/dt and not an OLS fit over every free-time sample. The same POVM's free-time curves are shown in the plot, distinct from the red four-point regression line.

Exact reduction for this objective: span of the four pure sampled states has rank r<=4. Twirling each POVM effect over all unitaries on its orthogonal complement preserves the four probabilities, all four effect volumes, PSD and completeness. Thus **without restricting the allowed POVMs** optimize `E_i=F A_i F†+alpha_i(I-FF†)`, with 4x4 PSD `A_i` summing to `I4` and nonnegative alpha summing to 1. Projected ascent with exact HS projection (Dykstra on 4x4 effects and simplex projection on alpha), 12 independent random starts for each d, up to 1100 updates per start, seed 20261126+d. Strict final positivity from 1e-7 uniform-effect mixture. Several starts stopped earlier at a local optimum or reached iteration limit; no global upper bound/certification. Finite-direction analytic-gradient checks on random interior effects using h=1e-7 give relative error ~1.90e-8 (d64) and ~7.65e-8 (d768).

Feasibility: min eigenvalues of reduced PSD blocks >=2.45e-8 (d64), >=2.50e-8 (d768); 4x4 completeness errors ~2.22e-16, complement weights sum to 1. Direct compact-state entropy evaluation reproduces 4-point objective/fit. Spectral edge mass in outer 20% of momentum range for d768 at kicks 0,1,2,3: ~1e-32,2e-29,2e-28,1e-10; for d64 after first kick ~0.757, after third ~0.311. Thus d64 numerical result is not a faithful unbounded-momentum K10 rotor. Corrected d768 calculation required increasing the cutoff without changing hbar_eff; it uses no d=768 relation hbar=2pi/768.

**Interpretation:** The optimal four-point fit largely exploits an abrupt entropy transition between kick 1 and kick 2 followed by near saturation; the red least-squares line is a poor model of the continuous free-time curves. A large finite-time optimized slope does **not** establish sustained entropy production, a chaos indicator, or Pesin equality. This unrestricted POVM is tailored to four known orbit states. These values are attained lower bounds on the full-POVM slope maxima, not certified global maxima. The short four-kick fit is also not a pre-Ehrenfest comparison across j/hbar.
