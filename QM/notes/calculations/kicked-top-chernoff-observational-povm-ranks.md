# Kicked-top Chernoff observational entropy: alternative POVMs and ranks

[Numerically tested] `calculations/code/kicked_top_chernoff_observational_povm_ranks.py`; figure `calculations/results/kicked_top_chernoff_observational_povm_ranks_j30_100_states.png` (visually inspected). The script also generates a matching `.npz` when run. Spin j=30 (d=61), seed 20261116, 100 initial states and k=2,4,5,6, t=0,...,5. Evaluate the user's **classical outcome Chernoff** entropy, averaged over 100 states (not the entropy of the mean state). At t=0 evaluate p without backaction; thereafter apply U_k=D_kR, apply the same fixed POVM with outcomes discarded, and evaluate the resulting p. For projectors P_i use Lüders dephasing \(\sum_iP_i\rho P_i\); for the nonprojective case use \(\sum_i\sqrt{E_i}\rho\sqrt{E_i}\). All ranks refer to effect/projector ranks, not the rank of the evolving state.

Measurements: Jz rank-one control; Jz projectors onto consecutive blocks of up to 4 eigenvectors; Jx rank-one projectors; Jx projectors onto blocks of up to 4 or 8 eigenvectors (the last rank is 1 or 5, respectively, at d=61); and full-rank unsharp Jz effects \(E_m=0.35P_m+0.65I/61\). For a block P_i, reference probabilities are \(q_i=\operatorname{Tr}(P_i)/61\), including unequal last-block weight. For unsharp effects each \(q_m=1/61\). [Proved] Expanding the Lüders Kraus operators \(\sqrt{E_m}=aI+(b-a)P_m\) gives the channel \(\lambda\rho+(1-\lambda)\mathcal D_z(\rho)\), where \(a=\sqrt{0.65/61}\), \(b=\sqrt{0.35+0.65/61}\) and \(\lambda=61a^2+2a(b-a)\).

[Numerically tested] The rank-one Jz curves coincide as predicted analytically; the other POVMs produce different k-dependent curves at finite times. Thus the fixed rank-one Jz no-go **does not extend to all fixed POVMs or other ranks**. Sensitivity to k is a finite-size measurement-specific finding, not proof that the entropy universally classifies classical chaos. The graph uses one shared vertical scale; at late times it approaches the ceiling \(\log61\), reducing contrast. Following the user's plotting preference, the plot displays only curves and measurement labels, without an additional difference statistic.

Checks: normalization of the 100 input states, Floquet unitarity, trace preservation, sample PSD after each step (first state), entropy bounds, exact two-outcome delta/uniform test including the s=0 support convention; the rank-one Jz channel is evaluated via its analytically equivalent rotation-only form to avoid floating-point support artifacts. For the other Jz measurements the first-kick outcome distribution is computed from rotation alone (the final diagonal twist cannot change those probabilities); the postmeasurement density matrix still retains the twist where the instrument permits it. Python 3.13.17, NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.16.3, Matplotlib 3.10.8; run with `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, `MPLBACKEND=Agg`. Finite ensemble, j=30, five kicks; no confidence interval or shot noise simulation. The shared `calculations/code/chernoff_utils.py` evaluates the classical Chernoff minimization and checks the endpoint convention.
